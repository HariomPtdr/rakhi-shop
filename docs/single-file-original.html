    06 — Questions

    Before you ask

    Will it reach before Raksha Bandhan?

    —

    How do I pay?

    UPI is easiest — we send a QR or UPI ID on WhatsApp once you send your bill. Cash on delivery also works; the courier charges a small extra fee for it.

    Will it look exactly like the photo?

    These are tied by hand, so bead placement and shade vary a little. We send a photo of your actual rakhi on WhatsApp before packing, and ship only after you say yes.

    Some designs show a drawing instead of a photo.

    Those are in stock but not photographed yet, so they are marked "Drawing". The drawing uses the real thread, bead and centre piece. Ask on WhatsApp and we will send you a photo before you order.

    Can you ship abroad?

    Yes, but it costs more and takes longer. Tell us the country at least three weeks ahead and we will quote the shipping.

    I need five of the same design.

    Change the quantity in the cart. Above fifty pieces the bulk rate applies.

    What if something is wrong with the order?

    If it arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, send a photo on WhatsApp within two days and we will replace it or refund you. Rakhis with a printed name cannot be returned.

    Still not answered?

    Message us. We usually reply within a few hours.
